# Paper corpus — knowledge digest

One entry per paper in this folder, grouped by theme. **Curated, not generated** — keep it
in sync after each fetch (`python3 research/papers/corpus_status.py` lists the gaps). An
entry cites its PDF by filename, which is how coverage is checked.

Entry format:

```markdown
- **<Title>** (<year>) — `<file.pdf>`
  - *Finding:* what the paper actually shows, at what effect size, on what data.
  - *For this project:* the module or milestone it informs, and how.
```

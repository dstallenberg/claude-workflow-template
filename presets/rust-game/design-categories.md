### Missing Core Loops
- Is the **core gameplay loop** clearly defined? (moment-to-moment → session → long-term progression)
- Are there enough **reasons to keep playing** after the first hour? After 10 hours?
- What does the **endgame** look like? Is there one?

### Mechanical Gaps
- Systems that are referenced but never detailed (e.g., "crafting" mentioned but no recipe design, progression curve, or UI flow)
- Interactions between systems that haven't been thought through (e.g., how does weather affect combat? How does stealth interact with base building?)
- Missing **feedback loops** — does the player know why they died? Can they learn from mistakes?
- **Balance concerns** — are there obviously dominant strategies? Is there a reason to use melee over ranged (or vice versa)?

### Player Experience
- **Onboarding** — how does a new player learn the game? Is there a tutorial? Are mechanics discoverable?
- **Pacing** — is there downtime between tension? Does danger escalate meaningfully?
- **Social dynamics** — what incentivizes cooperation vs competition? Can players trade? Form groups?
- **Frustration points** — full loot drop + random spawn = rage quit? How is this mitigated?

### Missing Systems
- Economy/trading
- Communication (chat, emotes, signals)
- Progression persistence across deaths
- Map/world knowledge systems
- Group/clan mechanics
- Server rules/modding/custom servers
